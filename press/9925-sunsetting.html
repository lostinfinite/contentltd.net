Sunsetting

ContentLTD, The ContentLTD Brand, sunbunni  |  September 2025

Hello,

 

I am ContentLTD, the Founder at the ContentLTD Brand. ContentLTD started as a brand that originally marketed ordinary people’s ER;LC servers that are just beginning. To give ordinary people the tools to market their ER;LC server, however, as the months moved by, we started to advance our mission, our goal, and our objectives, and we slowly moved into what we are today. However, we have discovered that those changes would take a lot more maintenance and resources than we had. We didn’t have the staff, and barely had the funding; however, we tried to go with Anti-raid despite this. 

 

During our time as a “Server Management Agency,” we ran into many controversies, and we recovered, assessed our ways, and changed how we conduct ourselves. We found ourselves having a lack of Resources and actually active staff members. Furthermore, I have also found a lack of time for the brand within the past few months, along with a decline in interest in the brand. Recently, I have decided to ask myself and the Senior High Ranking team 1 question. “Is what we are doing working?” And the answer was honestly no. I then thought about ways to change that answer, to no avail.

 

With that said, we will Permanently Sunset on 9/16/25. We appreciate all of our staff, contributors, investors, creators, partners, and affiliated servers for their continued support, but more importantly, Thank You for supporting us and our mission to improve ER;LC.
